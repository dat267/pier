# Review timeline

Chronology of the audits and release gates behind this port. Each finding was
reproduced through a supported boundary before its fix, repaired red-first, and
committed separately with its reference citation and evidence in the commit
message. `TODO.md` is the live ledger; it is empty when nothing is unresolved.

Reference for every window: read-only pi v1.4.0 (`4a080252`), verified with
`just reference`. Full per-window ledgers, reproductions and coverage notes are
in git history before `b9a3688`; the design narrative lives in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and the intentional differences in
[docs/DIVERGENCES.md](docs/DIVERGENCES.md).

## Shared gates

Every window ran `just check`, `just cross` (six cross-build/vet targets) and
`just reference`, plus a full `go test -count=2 ./...` with a freshly built
`PIER_TEST_BIN` where a fix touched several packages. Fixture corpora are
regenerated by executing the pinned TypeScript and compared byte-for-byte (65
offline corpora, 15 interactive golden families). The race detector cannot run
locally (`CGO_ENABLED=0`); CI runs `go test -race -count=2 -timeout 300s ./...`
and is the authority for race freedom.

## 2026-10-24 — five-hour parity audit

Baseline `2fc19f6` (v0.0.35). Cloudflare auth field presence and cancellation,
Azure deployment rewrite bytes/nil payload hooks, MCP token-auth truthiness and
`resource=` handling, agent null-header forwarding, tool-hook failure and
cancellation precedence, Anthropic SSE line boundaries, and Markdown
autolinks/angle anchors. Every fix had source-generated fixtures; fuzzing
covered seven targets (bounded runs, all passing). Ended with six-target
build/vet, full suite, and an explicit note that the review was time-bounded,
not exhaustive.

A release is tagged only after CI passes on the exact target commit and the
release workflow produces all seven binaries plus checksums; `pier update`
consumes those assets. macOS binaries are ad-hoc signed (arm64) or unsigned
(amd64), and neither is notarized.

## Coverage limits that persist

Not cleared by any window: `internal/highlight` beyond its API, deep
`tui/editor|latex|keys` and selector paths, most of `ai/bedrock_converse` and
`ai/mistral_conversations` beyond their corpora, print-mode boot, the theme
loader and HTML template as fuzz targets, cold-filesystem or MCP-enabled startup
performance, and race freedom outside CI. Passing tests, differential fixtures
and static review cannot certify the absence of defects.
